1.Cheese Pizza

About the Recipe : Cheese pizza is a popular Italian dish loved by people around the world. It is prepared with pizza dough, tomato sauce, cheese, and vegetables. It is a delicious choice for snacks and parties.
Preparation Time: 30 Minutes

Ingredients:

  • 1 pizza base
  • ½ cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup grated mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup chopped capsicum
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • ¼ cup chopped tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon oregano

Instructions:

  1. Place the pizza base on a clean plate or tray.
  2. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the base.
  3. Add grated mozzarella cheese.
  4. Spread the chopped vegetables over the cheese.
  5. Sprinkle oregano on top.
  6. Bake the pizza in a preheated oven at 200°C for about 10–15 minutes, until the cheese melts and the base is cooked.
  7. Allow it to cool slightly, then slice and serve.

Veg Burger

About the Recipe: A veg burger is a tasty snack made with a soft bun, vegetable patty, and fresh vegetables. It can be served with tomato ketchup or your favorite sauce.
Preparation Time:25 minutes

Ingredients:

  • 2 burger buns
  • 2 vegetable patties
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons tomato ketchup

Instructions:

  1. Cut the burger buns into two halves.
  2. Lightly toast the buns.
  3. Cook the vegetable patties according to the package instructions.
  4. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the inside of the buns.
  5. Place lettuce, the vegetable patty, tomato slices, and onion slices on the bottom half.
  6. Cover with the top half of the bun.
  7. Serve the burger with ketchup.

White Sauce Pasta

About the Recipe: White Sauce pasta is a popular dish prepared with boiled pasta, milk, cheese, and vegetables. It is a comforting meal that can be enjoyed for lunch or dinner.
Preparation Time:25 Minutes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup pasta
  • ½ cup milk
  • ¼ cup grated cheese
  • ½ cup chopped capsicum
  • ¼ cup chopped onion
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Boil the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Drain the pasta and keep it aside.
  3. Heat butter in a pan.
  4. Add chopped onion and capsicum, then cook until slightly soft.
  5. Add milk, grated cheese, salt, and black pepper.
  6. Stir until the sauce becomes creamy.
  7. Stir until the sauce becomes creamy.
  8. Serve warm.

Chocolate Pancakes

About the Recipe: Pancakes are soft and fluffy breakfast treats made from a simple batter. They can be served with honey, chocolate syrup, or fresh fruits.
Preparation Time: 20 Minutes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ¾ cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla essence
  • Chocolate syrup for topping
  • Chocolate chips (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Take a large bowl and add flour, cocoa powder, sugar, and baking powder.
  2. Mix all the dry ingredients well.
  3. Add milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla essence.
  4. Mix everything until you get a smooth batter. Do not overmix.
  5. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium-low heat.
  6. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan.
  7. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface.
  8. Flip the pancake carefully and cook the other side until done.
  9. Repeat with the remaining batter.
  10. Serve the pancakes with chocolate syrup and chocolate chips.
  11. Serve warm with chocolate syrup, sliced bananas, strawberries, or your favorite fruits.

Cooking Tips For Beginner

  • Always read the recipe before starting to cook.
  • Wash vegetables and fruits before using them.
  • Measure ingredients carefully.
  • Keep your cooking area clean.
  • Be careful when using hot utensils.
